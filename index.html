1. WhatsApp Scam Message / Call

Block: Chat > Block + Report Spam | Report in-app

How to Block ▾

• Open suspicious WhatsApp chat > Tap the 3 dots (top right) > Tap More > Tap Report > Check "Block contact and delete chat".

• This submits the last 5 messages to WhatsApp's trust & safety team to ban the perpetrator's number permanently.

📱
2. SMS Scam (Customs, KYC, Electricity, Toll)

Block: Forward to 1909 | Report: cybercrime.gov.in / 7726

How to Block ▾

• India: Forward SMS to 1909 (TRAI DND Fraud registry) or report on Chakshu portal (sancharsaathi.gov.in).

• USA / UK: Forward suspicious text messages to 7726 (SPAM) free of charge to alert mobile carriers.

📧
3. Email Phishing (Bank, PayPal, Netflix, Amazon)

Block: Gmail > Report Phishing | Report: phishing@bank.com

How to Block ▾

• Click the 3 dots inside Gmail / Outlook > Select Report Phishing. Never click unsubscribe links inside scam emails.

• Forward the original email as an attachment to your bank's fraud inbox or reportphishing@apwg.org.

4. Instagram, Facebook & Telegram Fake Profile

Block: Profile > Block | Report: In-App Scam / Impersonation

How to Block ▾

• On Telegram: Tap chat profile > ... > Report > Fake Account / Scam.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ) & People Also Ask

Direct, expert-verified answers to help you spot and report SMS, WhatsApp, link, number, and email scams.

1. How to tell if a message is a scam? +

To tell if a message is a scam, check for extreme urgency (e.g., account closure in 24 hours), requests for OTP or passwords, unknown links with lookalike domains (such as amaz0n-support.com), and prizes that sound too good to be true. Paste the text into IsThisScamOrReal.com to instantly scan for fraud signals.

2. Is this SMS or WhatsApp message a scam? +

Copy and paste your SMS or WhatsApp message into IsThisScamOrReal.com. Our 5-layer security scanner checks for phishing links, sender number spoofing, fake KYC warnings, customs parcel traps, and urgency triggers in English, Hindi, and Urdu.

3. How to check if a link is safe before clicking? +

Never click unknown links directly. Paste the URL or full message into our Link Checker tool to detect shortened links (bit.ly, tinyurl), typosquatted domains, and newly registered phishing websites.

4. Is this phone number a scammer? +

Use our Number Lookup tool to check unknown callers, international WhatsApp codes (+92, +62, +84, +44), and suspicious SMS sender headers against reported scam patterns.

5. What to do if I clicked a scam link or shared bank details? +

Disconnect from the internet immediately, call your bank to freeze your cards and UPI/netbanking, change your passwords from another device, and report the incident to official cyber authorities (Dial 1930 or visit cybercrime.gov.in in India, reportfraud.ftc.gov in the USA, or actionfraud.police.uk in the UK).

6. How to report a scam message anonymously? +

Use our Report a Scam section below to log the scam category, platform, and message pattern without sharing any personal data or OTPs.

7. Can a scammer hack my phone if I just open an SMS? +

Simply reading a text message will not hack your phone. The danger happens if you click a malicious link, download an unknown .apk file, or reply with an OTP or personal PIN.

8. Do banks ever ask for OTP or KYC update via an SMS link? +

No. Legitimate banks (SBI, HDFC, ICICI, Chase, Barclays, etc.) never ask you to share an OTP, PIN, or CVV, nor do they send third-party links threatening immediate account suspension within 24 hours.

9. How to check if a Telegram or work-from-home job offer is real? +

Real employers never ask you to pay a registration fee, security deposit, or "prepaid task" amount to unlock your salary. Paste the job message into our Job Offer Scan tool to check instantly.

10. How to check if an online shopping website is legit or fake? +

Watch out for luxury items or smartphones offered at 80–90% discounts, missing physical contact addresses, and stores that only accept irreversible bank transfers. Select the Shop Checker pill above to scan any store URL or offer.

11. What is the latest scam in 2026? +

The fastest-growing threat in 2026 is AI Voice Cloning and Deepfake Emergency Calls, where criminals use 3-second audio samples from social media to impersonate loved ones or bosses demanding urgent wire transfers. Other major 2026 threats include Quishing (malicious QR code stickers on parking meters), Digital Arrest video intimidation calls, and fake 90% off Stanley/Owala tumbler warehouse clearance stores. Browse our 150+ Scam Database for full details.

12. What are the biggest scams of the last 10 years (2016–2026)? +

Online fraud has evolved across distinct annual waves: 2016: IRS phone impersonations; 2017: Fake ICO crypto bubbles; 2018: SIM swap hijackings; 2019: Gift card payment extortions; 2020: COVID-19 relief and puppy courier traps; 2021: WhatsApp Pink malware APKs and predatory instant loan apps; 2022: Pig Butchering crypto romance schemes; 2023: Student loan debt relief fraud; 2024: Telegram task scam syndicates; 2025: Highway toll smishing and Digital Arrests; and 2026: AI deepfakes and quishing. You can filter all 150+ scams by year in our Timeline View.

13. What was the biggest scam in 2020 and 2021? +

In 2020, fraudulent COVID-19 stimulus check links, PPE mask webstores, and pet adoption shipping fees accounted for millions in losses. In 2021, rogue Android loan apps blackmailing users with hacked contact lists and WhatsApp Pink theme trojans infected millions of mobile devices before being banned by cyber authorities.

Ultimate Guide: How to Check if Any Message Is a Scam

TL;DR Summary: Never trust urgent threats, verify the exact domain name letter-by-letter, never share OTPs or PINs, and paste any suspicious SMS, WhatsApp, or email into IsThisScamOrReal.com before clicking.

1. Inspect the Sender ID & Domain

Legitimate organizations rarely use personal mobile numbers for official notices, nor do they use lookalike domains like amaz0n-support.com or sbi-kyc-online.top.

2. Look for Manufactured Urgency

If a message demands action within 24 hours to prevent account closure, SIM deactivation, or legal arrest, pause. Banks and government bodies give formal written notices.

3. Never Share OTPs, UPI PINs, or Screen Sharing

No customer support agent, bank manager, or delivery driver will ever legitimately ask for your One-Time Password (OTP) or ask you to install AnyDesk/TeamViewer.
